A conformance pack is a collection of AWS Config rules and remediation actions that you deploy
as a single entity, either in one account and Region or across an organization in
AWS Organizations. An aggregator collects AWS Config configuration and compliance data from
multiple accounts and Regions into a single account and Region.

## What this step covers

- What goes into a conformance pack
- How aggregators collect data, and why they're read-only
